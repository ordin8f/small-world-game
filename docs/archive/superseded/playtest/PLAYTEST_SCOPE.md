# Early Playtest Scope

The shareable build is intentionally one short episode in the middle of the larger game, not a promise of the complete product.

Included:

- one compact courtyard and playground;
- one observe, retrieve, return, join, and go-home loop;
- child-height camera and movement;
- one Emotional Lens arc;
- subtitles, sound toggle, reduced motion, restart, and local feedback export;
- static browser deployment.

Not included:

- full-week progression;
- character customization;
- final art, animation, or voice acting;
- mobile controls, gamepad support, accounts, multiplayer, cloud saves, analytics, or telemetry;
- production PlayCanvas architecture.
