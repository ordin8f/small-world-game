# The Lost Ball — Early Community Build

This release is a short middle episode rather than the complete Small World game.

The child recognizes the playground group but still stands at its edge. When the ball escapes into a quiet garden, retrieving it creates a small invitation to join.

The build is intended to test:

- child-height navigation and environmental scale;
- whether the social action feels meaningful rather than transactional;
- whether the Emotional Lens changes perception without exposing an emotion score;
- whether players understand the route without a minimap or arrow trail;
- whether the ending creates interest in another afternoon.

The characters, geometry, sounds, and animation remain placeholders. Community feedback should focus primarily on scale, mood, readability, pacing, and desire to continue.
