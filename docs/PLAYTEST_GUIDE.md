# Community Playtest Guide

## What to send testers

Send only the GitHub Pages URL and this instruction:

> Play once without trying to be efficient. Notice when the same place begins to feel different. At the end, copy the playtest notes and send them back with anything else you want to say.

## Questions worth asking

1. Did you understand what to do without a minimap or arrow trail?
2. Did the playground and home route feel large from the child's height?
3. When did the mood change, if at all?
4. Did retrieving the ball feel like a social action or merely an errand?
5. Was the episode too slow, too short, or appropriately quiet?
6. Did the children feel inviting, indifferent, or artificial?
7. What did you expect to be able to interact with but could not?

## What not to over-interpret

The prototype uses geometric characters, generated tones, simple collision, no facial animation, and no production art. Feedback about emotional timing, scale, readability, and desire to continue is more valuable than feedback about model quality.
