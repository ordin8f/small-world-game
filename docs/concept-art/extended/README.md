# Extended concept art

Generated 2026-08-28 to make the art direction gradeable.

`../visual-direction-contact-sheet.jpg` — the original human-authored reference —
survives only as a **320×155 thumbnail**. It is enough to read intent from, and
not enough to grade a rendered frame against. These nine plates (1672×941)
regenerate and extend its six named compositions at a usable resolution.

They were produced by GPT-5.6 with the five legible panels of the contact sheet
attached as composition/palette/lighting reference, prompted from
[`../../ART_DIRECTION.md`](../../ART_DIRECTION.md). They are a **derivative
reference target, not the original art** — where they and `ART_DIRECTION.md`
disagree, the document wins.

## How these are used

`DEMO_PLAN.md` Gate 1: `godot/tools/shots.ps1` drives the six named beats and
writes PNGs; those frames are compared against these plates **by a human** before
any asset work begins. The relevant plate for each beat is listed in the plan.

The gap to close is not detail — it is **value composition**: deep foreground
shadow, a luminous centre, the child reading as a shape rather than a face, and
enclosure rather than open field.
