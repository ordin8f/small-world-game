# Community Feedback Triage

Group feedback by evidence rather than by the number of words in a comment.

## High-priority signals

- players cannot understand where to go;
- the garden opening or another route is physically blocked;
- controls feel reversed, unstable, or inaccessible;
- players do not understand why returning the ball matters;
- the episode cannot be completed without debug controls;
- emotional effects cause discomfort or obscure essential information.

## Product-learning signals

- the world does or does not feel large from the child's height;
- the same route feels different before and after social acceptance;
- the quiet pacing feels involving, empty, or over-directed;
- players want another afternoon and can say what they hope to experience.

## Lower-priority prototype signals

- requests for final character models, facial animation, voice acting, or detailed textures;
- requests for multiplayer, accounts, achievements, mobile controls, or a larger map before the central loop is validated.

Summaries should preserve disagreement. Do not convert mixed feedback into a false consensus.
