# Agent Handoffs After the Early Build

The first playable episode is integrated. Further work should remain divided into bounded tracks.

## World and camera

Refine child-scale framing, route readability, camera collision, and low-end performance without changing narrative progression.

## Gameplay

Harden interaction recovery, object reachability, restart behavior, and deterministic state transitions without rewriting dialogue or art direction.

## Emotional Lens

Tune comfort, energy, curiosity, and their visual/audio outputs without adding a visible emotion score or changing physical collision.

## Narrative and social

Refine timing, gestures, objectives, and short dialogue so belonging is conveyed through action rather than explanation.

## Accessibility and QA

Expand browser coverage, keyboard/focus testing, reduced motion, subtitles, mute behavior, and performance checks.

## Release and community testing

Maintain Pages deployment, prepare tester instructions, collect copied feedback notes, and summarize recurring findings without silently adding telemetry.

## Production port

Only after playtest evidence supports the mechanics, define the boundary for moving validated systems into PlayCanvas and strict TypeScript.
